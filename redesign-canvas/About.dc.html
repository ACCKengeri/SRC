how we began

Association of Christian Christites is a community of all Christian students studying in Christ (Deemed To Be University). We spread the message of Jesus, encourage fellowship, and support each other in leading fulfilling Christian lives, striving to make a meaningful difference in the world.

ACC creates a strong bond of friendship and community among students. Our core vision is to build a supportive, faith-filled atmosphere on campus that glorifies God. We organise regular activities, including Holy Mass, Leadership Training Programmes, spiritual retreats, cultural fests, feast celebrations, outreach initiatives, and daily intercessory prayers. The ACC community includes over 4,500 members spanning five campuses.

Every day, members meet at the Chapel of St. Kuriakose Elias Chavara to pray together for our university, the student community, the world, and special intentions. A monthly Holy Mass is organised on the First Friday of every month, prepared cooperatively by students from various academic departments.
