Liturgy

Behind the scenes, building up worship.

The Liturgy Team is the backbone of ACC, orchestrating sacred ceremonies and guiding through spiritual rituals with reverence and care. From coordinating prayers and leading intercessory sessions to organising Holy Mass, we ensure meaningful worship experiences.

what this domain contributes

Facilitating Spiritual Growth

The Liturgy Team provides guidance and support during religious ceremonies, fostering an environment for individuals to deepen their spiritual connections and grow in faith.

Promoting Unity

Through their organisation of communal worship and care, the Liturgy Team helps strengthen bonds within the community, promoting a shared sense of purpose.

Emotional Support

Beyond their formal duties, members actively listen to individuals, offering emotional support and guidance during times of spiritual reflection and personal struggle.

A Sense of Belonging

By creating inclusive spaces for confession and prayer, the Liturgy Team cultivates a sense of belonging for all members, regardless of background or circumstance.
