LiturgyBehind the scenes, building up worship.–

The Liturgy Team is the backbone of ACC, orchestrating sacred ceremonies and guiding through spiritual rituals with reverence and care. From coordinating prayers and leading intercessory sessions to organising Holy Mass, we ensure meaningful worship experiences. Beyond our formal duties, we offer compassionate support, fostering a sense of belonging.

  • Facilitating Spiritual Growth
    Guidance and support during religious ceremonies, an environment to deepen spiritual connections and grow in faith.
  • Promoting Unity
    Organising communal worship and care that strengthens bonds and a shared sense of purpose.
  • Emotional Support
    Actively listening to individuals, offering guidance during spiritual reflection and personal struggle.
  • A Sense of Belonging
    Inclusive spaces for confession and prayer, for every member regardless of background.
