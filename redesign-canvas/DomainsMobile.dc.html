Liturgy

Behind the scenes, building up worship.

–

The Liturgy Team is the backbone of ACC, orchestrating sacred ceremonies with reverence and care — coordinating prayers, leading intercessory sessions, organising Holy Mass, and offering compassionate support.

  • Facilitating Spiritual Growth
    Support during ceremonies; an environment to grow in faith.
  • Promoting Unity
    Communal worship that strengthens bonds.
  • Emotional Support
    Listening and guidance in personal struggle.
  • A Sense of Belonging
    Inclusive spaces for confession and prayer.
